PUBLICATION

Additive-Free Size Tuning of Monodisperse ZIF-8 Particles via Fluidic Control for Advanced Colloidal Architectures

30 July 2026

Abstract summary. An additive-free fluidic-control strategy regulates monodisperse ZIF-8 particles while preserving the adsorption capacity needed for responsive colloidal crystal architectures.

Article DOI: 10.1039/D6CC04499AFront Cover DOI: 10.1039/D6CC90283A

PUBLICATION

Production of Gold Nanoparticle-Containing Chitosan Microcapsules Using an Inkjet Mixing System and Control of the Absorption Wavelength

1 March 2026

Abstract summary. Inkjet mixing reduces a gold precursor and gels chitosan droplets to form gold nanoparticle-containing microcapsules. The study compares trade-offs among particle uniformity, encapsulation efficiency, and acid durability, and shows that thorn-growth conditions control the absorbance peak of sea-urchin-like gold nanoparticles.

Article DOI: 10.1016/j.cej.2026.174028

PUBLICATION

Room-Temperature Flow Synthesis of Pd–Ru Solid-Solution Alloy Nanoparticles Using Microreactor and Catalytic Performance of Carbon Monoxide Oxidation

8 July 2025

Abstract summary. A high-mixing microreactor enables room-temperature aqueous synthesis of Pd–Ru solid-solution alloy nanoparticles with uniform size and composition. Ru-induced local structural disorder provides optimal CO oxidation activity at a lower Pd composition than reported previously.

Article DOI: 10.1021/acs.chemmater.5c00747
